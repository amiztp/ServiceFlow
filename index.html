Seamless Service Booking & Nearby Technician Dispatch (Sri Lanka)

ServiceFlow connects commercial property managers in Colombo, Kandy, and Galle with certified local field technicians for fast HVAC, CCTV, Electrical, and Commercial repairs in LKR.

How ServiceFlow Works (4-Step Workflow)

  1. 1. Create Service Request

    Customers choose a category (HVAC, CCTV, Electrical, Solar) and describe the issue.

  2. 2. Instant Estimate & Quote (LKR)

    ServiceFlow calculates labor, material costs, and line-item estimates automatically in LKR.

  3. 3. Filter & Pick Up Nearby Job

    Certified local technicians filter available orders by proximity (within 5-15 km) and claim jobs.

  4. 4. Complete Work & Invoice

    Technician performs repairs, logs notes, customer signs off, and invoice is finalized.

Supported Field Service Categories

  • HVAC & Climate Control: Commercial Rooftop Units (RTU), R-410A Refrigerant, Compressor Reset
  • CCTV & Security Systems: 4K IP Dome Cameras, Cat6 STP Cabling, NVR Configuration
  • Electrical & Power: 400A Main Service Panels, Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) >50V, Surge Suppression
  • Commercial Refrigeration: Walk-in Coolers, Chiller Maintenance, Door Gaskets
  • Solar & Energy: Solar Inverters, Thermal Scans, String Testing, Panel Washing
  • Commercial Plumbing: Pressure Relief Valves, Industrial Piping, Pipe Leak Repair
